Drop your scanned yearbook photos in this folder (e.g. prom.jpg, game.jpg, grad.jpg).

Then open lib/site-config.js and list them in the `collage` array, e.g.:

  collage: [
    "/collage/prom.jpg",
    "/collage/game.jpg",
    "/collage/grad.jpg",
    ...
  ],

The homepage tiles them into a mosaic with "RIVERVIEW HIGH SCHOOL" across the middle.
Use 6–10 photos for the best look. Landscape photos work best for the wide tiles.
